Whisper prototype is finished and checked in a real browser at 390, 768 and 1440: prototypes/whisper/ (index.html, styles.css, shot-390.png, shot-1440.png). Not committed.

TYPE + PALETTE
- Newsreader (variable, optical size 72, weight 300) for the name, tagline, stats and sheet titles. Hanken Grotesk for the role line, body and UI. Fragment Mono only for measurements: the redline labels, the clock and the sheet numbers.
- Why: a light high-contrast serif set enormous is how this variant gets its typographic confidence without being loud. I expect Blueprint and Exposed to lead with a grotesk or mono display, so this is the clearest type difference. The name runs across the full width on one line at every breakpoint.
- Palette: warm proofing paper oklch(.968 .007 84), graphite ink, and one proofreader's red oklch(.52 .19 31) that only appears for backstage things (peek overlay, focus ring, the "Learning out loud" draft tag, the ambient light).
- Contrast is computed, not eyeballed: muted text 6.8:1, red 5.5:1, ink 15:1. The 3.6:1 grey is only used at display size.

LAYOUT (how it differs, not just quieter)
- Hero: huge name; the role line on one line under it; then an asymmetric row with the tagline in columns 1–7 (italic "behind") and the bio, buttons and text social links in columns 9–12.
- The stats row sits on hairline rules aligned to the grid, framed by the only crop marks in the hero. The whole hero fits in a 1440×900 viewport.
- The hairline grid is drawn from the same tokens as the layout (--cols, --gutter, --col-gap), so the lines run exactly along real content edges. It goes 12, 6 or 4 columns by breakpoint.
- Sheets: each sticky sheet sits one tab lower than the last (top = --space-5 + i × --sheet-tab). Stacked, they turn into an index: 01–04, title, tag.
- Each sheet has an inline stroke-only line drawing. Stroke color and width come from --illo-stroke and --line-illo, and dashed "ghost" strokes show what changed: an org chart mid-reorg held level, layers going tokens → components → page, a long winding route replaced by a direct path to a flag, a lab notebook with crossed-out trials and one circled.

AMBIENT MOTION
- A slow light passes across the column hairlines in the redline color, then rests (32s cycle, about 20s moving).
- It is the grid's own lines lit through a moving mask, driven by a registered custom property (@property --sweep). Pure CSS, no JS.
- Under prefers-reduced-motion it is switched off. I emulated reduced motion and confirmed both the light and the scroll fill stop.

PEEK BACKSTAGE
- Hover a block, focus something inside it, or press the "Peek backstage" toggle (aria-pressed, shows every overlay at once).
- The block gets a dashed red trim box with a light wash, plus a red label quoting its tokens.
- Font size, line height and width in the labels are read live from computed styles with a few lines of JS. The overlay reports what the page is actually made of at the current width, e.g. "h1 · --type-name 236px/0.86 · Newsreader 300, opsz 72 · 1328×217".
- Keyboard: the toggle is the full keyboard path. Focus alone only opens the peek on blocks that contain a link or button (actions, social links). Name, tagline, bio, stats and sheets need the toggle.

ONE LOCAL SCROLL EFFECT
- The section heading's ink fills in as it crosses the viewport (CSS scroll-driven animation, view() timeline). Where that isn't supported, or with reduced motion, it shows a static two-tone: "Four topics." in ink, the rest in grey.
