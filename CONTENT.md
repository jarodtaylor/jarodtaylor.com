## About

**Heading:** Same problem, bigger leverage.

I started in 2008 as an interface designer at The Dallas Morning News. Self-taught, no CS degree, lots of CSS. I've been chasing the same question ever since: how do you make it easier for a lot of people to build good things, fast?

First the answer was CSS frameworks and component libraries. Then design systems, which I've now founded at three companies. Then docs, tooling, and onboarding. Eventually it was the teams and the culture around all of it. The problem stayed the same. The leverage got bigger.

I've also spent 12+ years leading through the kind of change most leaders only see once. I was employee #11 at a healthcare startup that Aetna absorbed, then went through the CVS Health buyout. At JCPenney, I led a 40-person global org through Chapter 11, COVID, and furloughs. At Estée Lauder, I watched the entire C-suite turn over. I learned more from those years than from any smooth quarter.

And I have ADHD. It's a big part of why I care so much about systems: I need them more than most people do. It's also why I've gone all in on AI agents, which have become a kind of external executive function for me. I'm not an AI expert. I'm a leader learning it hands-on and writing down what I find.
